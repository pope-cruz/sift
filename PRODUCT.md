# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Students who receive important information across messages, documents, screenshots, links, places, and plans, and need to recover it later without adopting another dashboard or filing system.

## Product Purpose

sort — always lowercase, no suffix or stylization — is where a student sends the mess college arrives in: syllabus PDFs, screenshots, links, listings, and stray thoughts. sort works out what each thing is, sorts it into the right kind of context with its source attached, connects it to what was sorted before, and brings it back or acts on it when it matters. The landing page should let a first-time visitor understand the send → understand → sort → connect → bring back loop within 30 seconds, and distinguish the scripted page demonstration from the working messaging prototype.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and transparent. The experience should reduce anxiety around forgotten information, feel native to ordinary messaging behavior, and make bounded, evidence-backed claims rather than presenting the prototype as a finished consumer service.

## Anti-references

Do not position sort as a generic AI assistant, academic planner, chatbot, calendar replacement, or all-in-one productivity workspace. Do not lead with “memory,” “AI agents,” integrations, or a list of capabilities. Do not resemble a SaaS dashboard, productivity command center, or account-heavy web app. Avoid feature-grid boilerplate, inflated AI claims, opaque “magic” language, and visuals that imply the static simulation is live product functionality.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the behavior: users send sort the scattered things they receive, and sort makes them usable.
- Organize communication around the kinds of things people send and what sort does with them, never a feature checklist.
- Show concrete student scenarios instead of abstract feature claims.
- Practice prototype transparency: label simulations, limitations, storage, and model behavior honestly.
- Keep sort conversational and low-friction; messaging is the fastest way to send something, not the product itself.
- Preserve evidence and provenance wherever the interface presents extracted facts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA as the practical baseline: readable contrast, semantic structure, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, meaningful labels, and reduced-motion support. Use plain language and do not rely on color or animation alone to communicate state.
